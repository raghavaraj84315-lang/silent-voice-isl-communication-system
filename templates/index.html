How to Sign

Letters Mode: Hold a letter steady in front of the camera for about 1 second. Once accepted, it appears in your text.
Words Mode: Raise both hands into camera view to start a sign gesture. Perform the sign motion, then lower your hands. The system recognizes the word automatically when your hands drop!
Auto-Voice: When enabled, every recognized sign is immediately spoken aloud in the selected language.
